Privacy Policy for Evola

Effective Date: 24/02/2026

Welcome to Evola 🌸

Your privacy is extremely important to us. Evola is designed to provide a safe, respectful, and private health experience for teenagers (13+) and adults.

1. Information We Collect

Evola does not directly collect personal information such as your real name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

However, the app uses trusted third-party services that may automatically collect certain technical and usage information as described below.

2. Data You Enter in the App

Any name, nickname, age, notes, or content you enter inside the app is stored locally on your device only.

This data is not transmitted to our servers and is not accessible by us.

If you uninstall the app, locally stored data is permanently deleted from your device.

3. Photos & Avatars

If you choose to use a personal image or avatar inside the app, it is stored locally on your device only and is not uploaded to any external server.

4. Third-Party Services

Evola integrates the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob – for displaying advertisements
  • Firebase Analytics – for app usage analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics (if enabled) – for crash reporting and app stability

These services may automatically collect certain information, which may include:

  • Device information (device model, OS version)
  • App usage data (interaction events, session duration)
  • IP address (general location at country level)
  • Advertising ID or other device identifiers
  • Crash logs and diagnostics data

This data is collected and processed directly by these third-party providers according to their own privacy policies.

5. Advertising

AdMob may use device identifiers and usage data to provide personalized or non-personalized advertisements.

You can control personalized advertising from your device settings (Google Settings → Ads).

6. Analytics

Firebase Analytics helps us understand how users interact with the app in order to improve performance and user experience.

No personally identifiable information is intentionally collected by Evola through analytics.

7. Children's Privacy

Evola is intended for users aged 13 and older.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user information. Locally stored data remains under your control on your device.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
