🔒 Data Collection

Evola does not collect any personal data at all, including name, nickname, age, profile photo, avatar, or any other personal information.

📱 Data Storage

Any information you enter in the app is stored locally on your device only. No data is sent, uploaded, copied, or shared with any external servers or third parties, and not even the app itself can access your locally stored data.

🖼️ Photos & Avatars

You may choose to use a personal photo or an AI-generated avatar. Images are used solely for visual identity within the app and are never uploaded, stored externally, shared, or accessed by any party.

👤 Name & Age

The app may ask you to enter a name or nickname for in-app personalization only. You may choose any name or nickname you like, and it does not need to be real. Entering age is optional and is used only to enhance the interactive experience and provide age-appropriate content.

📢 Advertising & Third-Party Services

Evola may display advertisements provided by trusted third-party services such as Google AdMob. These services may collect limited, non-personal data as required to deliver ads, in accordance with their own privacy policies.

The app may also use Firebase services solely to deliver visual assets and basic app configuration. No personal user data is stored or processed by Evola through Firebase.

🛡️ Data Security

All data belongs entirely to you and remains stored locally on your device. When the app is deleted, all locally stored data is permanently removed.

🌱 Safe Content for Teenagers

All content within Evola is designed in accordance with public decency standards. The app does not contain any sensitive, explicit, or inappropriate material for teenagers.

📌 Consent

By using Evola, you agree to this Privacy Policy and understand that your data remains fully protected and stored locally on your device only.
